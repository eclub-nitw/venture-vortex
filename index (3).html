Round 1: Behind the Startup

Pick one startup from a curated list of 50 major Indian companies. Rather than a generic company profile, analyse the strategic choices and execution tactics that let it stand out from its competitors.

    What you submit

    • A comprehensive Startup Teardown Report or Deck covering the seven areas below.
    • Upload your final presentation deck or report directly on Unstop.
    • Full names and details of all team members clearly displayed on the title slide.

    How Round 1 is judged

    Depth of Research: accuracy, thoroughness, and quality of data collected about the company and market30
    Understanding of Differentiation & Journey: how well the team identifies the specific strategic choices that separated the startup from competitors25
    Quality of Critical Analysis & Insight: originality and sharpness of the strategic insights25
    Structure & Presentation: clarity, visual appeal, logical flow, and professional formatting of the deck or report20

    Read the brief on Unstop ↗

    Round 2: Strategy Build

    Shortlisted teams step into the shoes of founders and strategists, and dive deeper into the sector of the startup they analysed in Round 1.

    Task 1

    Gap & Problem Identification

    Identify major unaddressed pain points, market inefficiencies, or consumer gaps within that specific sector.

    Task 2

    Startup Idea Formulation

    Develop a brand-new, scalable startup concept to solve the identified gaps.

    Task 3

    Framework Application

    Use core entrepreneurial and business frameworks (e.g. Value Proposition Canvas, Lean Startup Model, TAM-SAM-SOM, Business Model Canvas) to structure and justify your pitch deck.

    Detailed submission instructions, evaluation guidelines and problem statements will be shared directly with shortlisted teams.

    Round 3: Boardroom Showdown

    30–31 Oct

    Grand Finale, live and offline at NIT Warangal during Technozion. 30 Oct 08:00 → 31 Oct 20:00 IST.

    Finalist teams pitch their Round 2 strategy live to a panel of industry experts, founders and faculty. Then comes the hot-seat defence and Q&A, where judges challenge assumptions, execution logic and strategic trade-offs under real boardroom pressure.

    Who can register?

    The event is open to all undergraduate and postgraduate students enrolled across colleges in India. Professionals are also allowed to participate.
